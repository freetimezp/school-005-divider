01 / UNDERSTAND THE ELEMENT

One line.
New direction.

The <hr> element marks a thematic change between sections of content. Think of a new topic in an article, a scene change in a story, or a pause between two ideas.

You can use CSS to change its appearance: the line's color, thickness, style, and the space around it.

✳

Remember: use <hr> when the content has a meaningful break. Use borders when you simply need a decorative edge or divider.

FIELD NOTES / 01 THE EDITORIAL

First, the thought.

Every page begins with an idea. Good typography helps the reader find its rhythm, follow the story, and understand what matters.

Then, a new chapter.

A well-placed divider signals that the conversation is moving somewhere new. It doesn't need to shout to be noticed.

Define the edge.

A border draws a visible boundary around an element.

CONTENT

Easy way to remember: margin = outside, padding = inside, border = edge. All three work together to create the box model.
